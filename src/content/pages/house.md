A house built in 1908, renovated one room at a time. Every room has its own theme, and I designed all of them except the kitchen’s original layout.

I've traveled the real world and explored countless fictional ones, even some of my own creation. I wanted to bring a sense of wonder to my own home and for each room to carry its own vibe, be its on world, without becoming a theme park. I think I'm succeeding...
