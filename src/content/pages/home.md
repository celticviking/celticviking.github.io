I’ve traveled the world, worked many jobs, and lived in a variety of locations, but never set down roots until recently. I was a weekend craftsman with no forge or shop of my own. Now I’m renovating a house built in 1908, am building out a forge and shop of my own, have a workbench, and a room for every world I wandered through on the way here.

This is where I keep my interests, musings, and projects. Have a look.
