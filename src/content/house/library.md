I wanted to step into a cozy Victorian library, warm and inviting. I thought about Mahogany at first, but decided on cherry when I inherited the cherry piano.

Nothing from 1908 except the shape of the room survives. The coffered cherry ceilings with two kinds of lighting were one of the best decisions I could have made.

The room is L-shaped, and seemed perfectly suited to hold my instruments and musical life, alongside my personal library. It's also the only room in the house with a table and chairs, because I'm supposedly an adult and that's something an adult should have.

I chose not to raise the ceilings in the room, for budgetary and schedule reasons. The roof trusses span the width of the room and redoing all of them would have taken too long and exceeded the budget by too much. In the end, the lower ceilings compared with the rest of the house add to the cozy-factor in the room.
