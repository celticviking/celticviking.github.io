I wanted the vibes of a medieval apothecary and alchemist's lab without sacrificing modern conveniences or feeling like a theme park.

Painting the ceiling brings the cozy, and the natural elements - plants, natural woods, copper and stone, bring it all together.

*I should've gone with taller cabinets but I ordered them before we'd removed the ceiling to discover 3 more feet of space. Future upgrade! Painting the ceiling (the first time I'd ever done it) was a risky call. It paid off so well I'm addressing the ceilings in every other room of the renovation, now.*
