I wanted to feel a sense of wonder, to feel like I'd finally made it in the world, on this acre I've carved out for myself. When we found 6 more feet (and two roofs...) above my roof, we raised the ceiling as high as we could. Entering from the library feels grand and invigorating.

I work from home, and being in a room with high ceilings, expansive space, and with all of my "toys" near but not within immediate reach became the right balance to breathe, focus, and look around in contentment.

We had to gut the floors clear through to the basement, they were so warped and uneven. We removed the ceiling and two roofs we found between it and my actual roof, gaining 6 feet of height in the process. I added beams, exposed the brick chimney, and put in a raised step for a home theater experience. Mounting the arcade cabinet to the wall was my inner child's icing on the cake. My consoles are hidden within the floating cabinet beneath the TV.
