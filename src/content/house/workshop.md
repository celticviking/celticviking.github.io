In my Library, I step back in time. In my Living Room & Office, I exist in the now. In my Home Gym, I'll step into the future. Here, I wanted to merge everything. Steampunk became a target vibe, without theming everything.

Thus became the results of my indoor workshop. Copper-tin ceiling, retired naval vessel copper lighting. Natural woods and verdigris cabinet hardware. Leatherworking station, tinkerer's desk with a soldering station, 3D printer and humidity controlled filament storage, and a place to relax and ponder my next project. The window is a ceiling LED panel that can be dimmed or provide bright "day" light.

I decided to keep the angle of the roof itself, rather than try for a flat ceiling, in order to achieve maximum height. It made the copper tin crown moulding an itneresting challenge, but it all paid off in the end.
